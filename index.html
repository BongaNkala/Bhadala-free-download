FAQ

Good questions

Is Bhadala really free?

Yes. Bhadala is free to download and use. It remains the property of Thee Landlord (Pty) Ltd, and the licence terms are published with each release.

Where is my payroll data stored?

On your own computer. Bhadala keeps payroll data locally and does not upload it to our servers.

Which tax year does it support?

Bhadala ships with 2026/27 tax table defaults. The tables are editable, so you can update brackets, rebates and limits when SARS changes them.

Does Bhadala submit returns to SARS?

No. Bhadala prepares payroll figures, payslips and summaries. You are responsible for submitting your returns to SARS and verifying all rates and obligations.

Can I run several companies?

Not yet. Bhadala currently handles one company at a time.

What do I need to run it?

Bhadala is a Windows desktop application. Download the installer from the GitHub release page and follow the instructions supplied with the release.
